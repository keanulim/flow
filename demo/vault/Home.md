# Welcome to the Flow demo

Flow is an AI chat built for staying in flow, plus a notes app. This is a demo: AI replies are simulated, and everything you do here is private to you and resets after a while.

## Try these

- [ ] Open **Chats** (the speech bubble above) and pick *How HTTPS keeps data private*. The highlighted text in the first reply opens a **side question** about it. The main thread never sees side questions.
- [ ] In a reply, highlight any text and press **⌘K** (Ctrl+K on Windows) to ask about it on the side.
- [ ] Look at the **map** under the chat list: the conversation is a tree, and your place is lit up.
- [ ] Press **Make note** on a chat to turn it, branches included, into a note.
- [ ] Open the note [[How HTTPS works]]. Click the highlighted words to see a note about them.
- [ ] Press the **λ** button (bottom right) on any note to try the note assistant. Ask it to "add a bullet" or "make a page".
- [ ] Change the look in **Settings** (bottom left).
